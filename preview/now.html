Current Work

NCCR Microbiomes flagship project

Scalable SynCom design for soil resilience

Co-leading the computational track of a multi-center, 4-year NCCR Microbiomes flagship project across ETH Zürich, University of Bern, and EAWAG. Goal: establish mechanistic rules for designing synthetic microbial communities (SynComs) that survive field deployment without loss of critical strains.

My team is building the computational infrastructure: constraint-based metabolic models, pangenomics analysis, and machine learning to identify trophic dependencies and niche-filling strains. The hypothesis is that obligate cross-feeding relationships predict SynCom stability.

AI & structural bioinformatics

GPU-accelerated protein annotation pipeline

The bottleneck: AlphaFold2 was cutting into metagenomic workflows. Going from 14,000 MAGs to 3D protein structures took 2+ months on conventional HPC.

The fix: Co-designed a pipeline using ProstT5 + Foldseek (rapid structural language model + fast 3Di matching). Result: 100× speedup. Two-month bottleneck → one week.

This is now the standard annotation tool for the lab. It's currently powering functional prediction across soil metagenomes for the NCCR project.
