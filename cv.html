Summary

Computational biologist and NCCR Microbiomes Flagship Project Lead with a Ph.D. from Cambridge and 7+ years of expertise in metagenomics, metabolic modeling, and structural bioinformatics. Proven track record of developing high-throughput computational products (e.g., metaGEM) and directing multi-lab consortia to establish predictive rules for synthetic community (SynCom) assembly. Applies data-driven systems biology to challenges in environmental resilience, soil health, and agricultural biotechnology.

Experience

Postdoctoral Researcher & NCCR Flagship Project Lead

ETH Zürich · Zürich, Switzerland · October 2024 – Present

  • Scientific leadership: Co-leading multi-center NCCR Microbiomes Flagship Project across 5+ labs. Designed the 4-year computational roadmap for resilient SynCom design.
  • AI & structural bioinformatics pipeline: Co-designing a scalable functional annotation pipeline; pivoted from AlphaFold2 to ProstT5 + Foldseek. GPU-Slurm cluster for sequence-to-3Di translation. 100× throughput; 2-month bottleneck → 1 week.
  • Data-driven SynCom design: Constraint-based metabolic modelling, pangenomics, ML to identify trophic interactions and niche-filling strains for plant-associated and environmental microbiomes.
  • Cross-functional management: Directing a team of computational PhDs and postdocs.
  • Mentorship: ETH Block Course, NCCR Winter School, EMBO Practical Course (Bangalore); supervised Oxford master's theses; 50+ international researchers trained.

Doctoral Researcher · Computational Biologist

University of Cambridge & EMBL Heidelberg · August 2019 – August 2024

  • Software product engineering: Released metaGEM open-source pipeline. Full product lifecycle. Scaled HPC workflows; automated terabyte-scale reconstruction of 14,000+ genome-scale metabolic models.
  • Mechanistic modelling & applied systems biology: Cheese-fermentation flavor formation (with Chr. Hansen); obligate cross-feeding in soil; C. difficile metabolic niches.
  • Multi-omics integration: Plastic-degrading enzymes (mBio), metabolic auxotrophies (bioRxiv), ecological dynamics (Nature Microbiology).
  • EMBL projects: Contributed to DD-DeCaF and ShikiFactory100 EU-funded consortia on metabolic engineering of microbial communities.
